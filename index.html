About SPOTTED

A Pinterest-style feed where the shopping links write themselves.

Creators on shoppable platforms spend hours finding and linking every product by hand. SPOTTED does that step automatically: you upload a look, the pieces are identified, and the searches to find them are ready.

What the results mean

Pieces and descriptions come from Claude reading the image. They are usually accurate about type, colour and cut, and less certain about fabric.

Brands are only named when a logo, monogram, label or unmistakable signature design is visible, with a confidence level. Otherwise the brand is left blank.

Shop links are live searches built from the description, so they show real products and prices from real shops. They find the item or the closest matches; an exact match is not guaranteed.

Google Lens gives exact visual matches for the whole image. Use “Search the whole look on Google Lens” and drop the same image there.

Privacy

SPOTTED never identifies people in photos. An image you scan is sent to Claude to be read and is only stored if you choose to post it to Discover, where everyone with access to this page can see it.

Your account

Signing in uses your claude.ai account, so SPOTTED never sees or stores a password. Your profile, saved pieces and scan history are private to you: other people using this page cannot read them. You can delete all of it from the Account page at any time.

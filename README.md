# spotted.

Upload any outfit or beauty picture, see every piece identified, find where to buy it, and save pieces into boards. A for you page recommends pieces based on your taste.

By Lilia Jdaa Maniari — individual final project, November 2026.

- Website (GitHub Pages): https://liliajdaa.github.io/spotted/
- Full app with AI scanning, Google sign-in and the community feed: https://claude.ai/artifact/6cHnkQjW45N5QwQGbEr2cb

## What works where

| Feature | This website | The app on claude.ai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design, hero video, all pages | yes | yes |
| Describe a piece → shop links | yes | yes |
| Boards and saved pieces | in this browser | on your account |
| For you page | yes (learns from this browser) | yes, plus AI picks |
| AI scanning of images | links to the app | yes |
| Sign in with Google, scan history, community feed | links to the app | yes |
